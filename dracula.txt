The legend of Dracula remains a dark shadow over the history of literature. Bram Stoker created a monster that would live forever in the minds of readers. The count lives in a decaying castle high in the mountains of Transylvania. He waits for the arrival of Jonathan Harker with a hunger that transcends time.
Dracula is a creature of the night who commands the wolves and the fog. He possesses the strength of twenty men and can vanish into the mist at will. His eyes glow with a red fire that speaks of ancient evils and forgotten sorrows. He is the king of the vampires and the master of the undead.

Van Helsing represents the light of science and faith against the darkness. He understands that the vampire is not merely a myth but a physical plague. To defeat Dracula one must use garlic and silver and the sacred cross. The battle between life and death plays out across the streets of London.
